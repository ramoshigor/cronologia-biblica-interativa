# Novas ilustrações — v0.6

Três cenas geradas com a habilidade de imagem integrada, em modo nativo, e inspecionadas antes da integração. São interpretações artísticas de passagens bíblicas, não retratos históricos ou reconstruções de sítios. As figuras e as paisagens não determinam as datas do catálogo.

Cada imagem tem versão WebP de 1536 × 1024 e variante de 800 × 533 para reduzir a transferência no celular. Originais preservados durante a criação; arquivos de produção em `public/media/v2/`.

## Prompts usados

### I17 — Débora

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: landscape editorial illustration for the Portuguese website Cronologia Bíblica Interativa. Create a painterly, richly textured cinematic illustration of Deborah hearing villagers under a large date palm in the hill country of ancient Israel, inspired by Judges 4:4–5. Deborah is seated, dignified adult Levantine woman in simple linen clothing, a handful of men and women listen with respectful attention. Wide composition, subjects in middle distance, rolling hills and small stone settlements behind, olive trees, golden afternoon light. Muted deep teal shadows, warm ochre and sand, soft sage greens; elegant museum book illustration. Historical interpretation, not a literal reconstruction. Landscape 1536x1024. No text, letters, labels, logos, watermark, modern objects, armor fantasy, glowing magic or halos. Do not make a collage.

### I18 — Rute

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: landscape editorial illustration for Cronologia Bíblica Interativa. Create a warm painterly cinematic illustration inspired by Ruth 2. Ruth, a young adult woman from ancient Moab with brown skin, wears simple linen layers and a modest head covering and gathers fallen barley stalks in a harvest field near Bethlehem, several distant reapers and a few small stone houses on rolling Judean hills. Ruth in the middle distance, view slightly above field level, abundant golden barley with rich detailed texture, calm mood, morning light. Museum book illustration, ochre, warm sand, sage green and deep teal shadows. Historically inspired interpretation. Landscape 1536x1024, one cohesive scene. No text, labels, letters, logos, watermark, modern objects, fantasy, halos or collage.

### I19 — Ensino em Éfeso

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: landscape editorial illustration for the website Cronologia Bíblica Interativa. Illustrate Priscilla and Aquila privately explaining the Scriptures to Apollos in an ancient first-century Ephesian courtyard, inspired by Acts 18:24–26. Three adults around a modest wooden table: an intelligent dignified woman and her husband gently discussing with a younger bearded man who listens attentively. Linen clothing and simple cloaks typical of the eastern Mediterranean. Small closed scrolls and clay lamp on the table, ivy and weathered stone, a distant coastal city beyond the courtyard. Warm painterly museum book illustration with subtle canvas grain, deep teal shadows, muted sage, ochre gold, natural late-afternoon light. Composition wide 1536x1024, people middle distance with environment visible, one cohesive scene. Interpretative scene, not portraits or exact historical reconstruction. No readable text or writing, no labels, logos, watermark, modern objects, fancy fantasy costume, halos or collage.
